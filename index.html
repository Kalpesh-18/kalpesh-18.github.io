I'm a Data Scientist at SLB working at the intersection of industrial engineering and machine learning. My core work: turning raw sensor telemetry from oilfield equipment into actionable failure predictions before downtime strikes.

My toolkit spans the full ML lifecycle — ETL with Polars, anomaly detection with HDBSCAN, survival analysis with PyTorch, and CI/CD deployment via Azure Pipelines and Docker. I believe clean data infrastructure is as important as any model.

Beyond predictive maintenance, I explore quantitative finance, transformer architectures, and LLM systems. I solve DSA problems in C++ on LeetCode, write about cloud computing on Hashnode, and believe in learning in public — giving back to the community that helped me grow.
